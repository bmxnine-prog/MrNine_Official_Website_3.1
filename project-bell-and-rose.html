Bell&Rose

Eagle and wave engraving · Case and bracelet

The project

A soaring eagle carried across a field of rolling waves.

The composition flows from the sculptural case onto the bracelet, pairing detailed eagle feathers with layered wave motifs. Darkened recesses and bright hand-cut surfaces give the narrative depth while framing the watch’s vivid blue dial.
