About Mr. Nine

A bespoke watch should tell its owner’s story.

Mr. Nine Engraving is an independent studio in Taiwan creating one-of-one, traditionally hand-engraved watches for collectors around the world.

The philosophy

Every commission begins with a conversation.

I believe a bespoke watch should tell its owner’s story. Every engraving is created exclusively for a single collector, with a composition designed specifically for its owner rather than a repeated pattern.

The objective is not decoration alone. It is to create a personal object that can carry a story, belief or memory for years to come.

The craft

No CNC. No laser engraving.

Only traditional hand engraving. Every line, background and detail is cut by hand, allowing the depth and rhythm of the work to respond directly to the architecture of the watch.

Each bespoke watch typically requires 100-200 hours of hand engraving. Projects involving precious-metal inlay require even more time.

I

One collector

Every commission is developed exclusively for one owner and one watch.

II

One composition

Each project begins with an original design rather than a reused pattern.

III

Entirely by hand

Designed and traditionally hand engraved by Mr. Nine in Taiwan.

A final thought

As unique as the person who wears it.

Thank you for taking the time to discover my work. I hope these pieces inspire you, and I look forward to creating something truly unique together.
