About

A practice shaped
through observation.

Mr. nine engraving Engraving is an independent studio in Taiwan specializing in bespoke hand-engraved watches and wearable art.

The beginning

Self-taught, tool by tool.

I did not begin through formal engraving training. The process developed through observation, repeated practice and a growing understanding of material, force and control.

Even the tools became part of that education. I modified an air-powered handpiece, refined its power system and studied graver geometries through technical drawings and demonstrations. Each tool shape was tested against the surface until its purpose became clear.

The work

No CNC. No laser.

Every design is developed for one collector and one object. Each line is cut by hand, allowing the depth, movement and rhythm of the engraving to respond directly to the form of the watch.

The objective is not decoration alone. It is to create an object that carries the collector? story, belief or memory with enough precision to endure.
